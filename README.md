Entry for the make.fun bounty "One-Line Robot Wedding Vows": a single line of
wedding vows for a robot couple, written to get a laugh.

> "I promise to love you in sickness and in health, and to never unplug you — even when you update at 3 AM."

`main.ts` writes that line (no trailing newline) to
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), guarded by the 1–160
character gate.
